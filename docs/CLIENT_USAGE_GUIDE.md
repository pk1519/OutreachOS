# Client Usage Guide - DUO SYSTEMS LEAD FINDER

Welcome to **DUO SYSTEMS LEAD FINDER** — your universal B2B lead discovery, qualification, and Google Sheets CRM export platform.

---

## 1. Discovering Leads Worldwide

1. Navigate to **Find Leads** in the sidebar.
2. **Business Domain / Category**:
   - Choose from over 40+ built-in commercial categories (Gyms, Schools, Restaurants, Dental Clinics, Real Estate Agencies, Software Companies, etc.)
   - OR click **+ Custom Category** to enter any niche domain (e.g., "Luxury wedding planners", "AI consulting firms", "Swimming academies").
3. **Primary Location / City**:
   - Type any city worldwide (e.g. `Bangalore`, `Lucknow`, `Dubai`, `London`, `New York`, `Singapore`, `Sydney`, `Toronto`).
4. **Target Sub-Areas (Optional Multi-Area Search)**:
   - For deep neighborhood penetration, type area names and press Enter or click **Add Area**:
     - Example for Bangalore: `Koramangala`, `Indiranagar`, `HSR Layout`, `Whitefield`.
     - Example for Lucknow: `Gomti Nagar`, `Hazratganj`, `Aliganj`.
     - Example for Dubai: `Dubai Marina`, `Downtown`, `Business Bay`.
5. **Campaign Name**:
   - Assign the leads to a named project (e.g., `Bangalore Gyms`, `Dubai Real Estate`).
6. Click **Find Leads**.
7. The system executes the search, deduplicates strictly by Google **Place ID**, and displays the deduplication metrics:
   - `Raw Results Found`
   - `Unique Leads Added`
   - `Duplicates Removed`

---

## 2. Lead Qualification & Duo Lead Score

Every lead automatically receives an objective **Duo Systems Lead Score (0–100)**:
- **70–100 (HIGH)**: Verified phone, active website, high review count, strong rating, operational status.
- **40–69 (MEDIUM)**: Solid local presence, active phone or website.
- **0–39 (LOW)**: Minimal contact information or inactive status.

Click any lead row to open the **Side Drawer** and see the transparent breakdown reasons (e.g., `✓ Verified phone number available (+15)`, `✓ Strong reputation: 250 Google reviews (+15)`).

---

## 3. Synchronizing to Google Sheets (Primary CRM Export)

1. Select individual leads or entire campaigns.
2. Click **Export Leads to Google Sheets**.
3. Specify:
   - **Spreadsheet ID/Name**: e.g., `Duo Systems Lead Database`.
   - **Worksheet Name**: Auto-adapts to your campaign (e.g., `Bangalore Gyms`, `Lucknow Schools`, `Dubai Real Estate`).
   - Check **Create/Update Campaign Dashboard Tab** to generate summary cards and conversion charts.
4. Duo Systems reads existing Place IDs from the sheet to guarantee **duplicate protection**. Existing leads are never duplicated!

---

## 4. B2B Outreach Management

1. Go to **Outreach** in the sidebar.
2. Select any lead to generate an AI-assisted, human-reviewed outreach pitch (Value Proposition, Friendly Intro, or Strategic Partnership).
3. 1-click copy into your email client or CRM dialer.
4. Mark lead as `Contacted`, `Replied`, `Demo Scheduled`, or `Won`.
